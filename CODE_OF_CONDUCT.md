# Code of Conduct

The goal of mindyourownbytes is to make practical file privacy accessible to
everyone. We want a welcoming, technically rigorous community.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful of different skill levels, backgrounds, and opinions.
- Critique ideas and code with evidence, not personal attacks.
- Assume good faith, but take security concerns seriously.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or unwelcome contact.
- Do not publish anyone's private keys, passwords, documents, or personal
  information, including material accidentally included in a bug report.
- Keep Discussions and Issues on topic; avoid spam or commercial promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close discussions, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when necessary. Decisions should be
proportionate, explained when appropriate, and applied consistently.

To report abuse, use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature. To reach the maintainer privately instead, open a
[private advisory](https://github.com/N-Bermparis/mindyourownbytes/security/advisories/new)
and say that it concerns conduct. Please do not publish sensitive reports in public issues.

This policy applies to project repositories, pull requests, discussions,
issue trackers, and official community spaces.
